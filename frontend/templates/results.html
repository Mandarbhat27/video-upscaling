Shared Cache Locality

Unlike discrete GPU offloading which loses ~320 ms in PCIe bus DMA transfers (Aydin et al. [3]), VisionBoost processes frames inside CPU L1/L2/L3 cache with sub-microsecond memory hops.

Section 1.4 Objectives

Delivers all deliverables committed in Section 1.4: Brightness (-50 to +50), Contrast/Gamma (0.5x to 2.0x), CLAHE Low-Light Night CCTV Boost, and Gaussian Spatial Denoising.

Surveillance RTSP / OSD

Resolves the CCTV domain focus of Silva et al. [5] via live RTSP IP camera decoding, 24/7 campus gate demo loop, and official RIT-CAM-01 security timestamp OSD overlays.

144.9 FPS Throughput

Exceeds the 30 FPS broadcast threshold by 4.8x on an Intel 12th Gen Core i5-12450H laptop CPU, proving practical deployability without dedicated workstation GPUs.
